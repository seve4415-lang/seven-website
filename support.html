❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Help Topics
How does SEVEN compare pharmacy prices? ▼
SEVEN connects directly with licensed pharmacies across Saudi Arabia in real time. We display live pricing side-by-side so you can select the most affordable and fastest delivery option.
How does prescription fulfillment work? ▼
Upload a photo or digital copy of your doctor's prescription inside the app. A licensed pharmacist reviews and validates the prescription before preparing and dispatching your medication.
What are your customer support working hours? ▼
Our support specialists are available Saturday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM (KSA Time). Inquiries submitted by email or WhatsApp outside these hours are resolved within 24 hours.
How can I request account or data deletion? ▼
You can initiate account deletion anytime by visiting our Account Deletion portal or emailing info@se-7en.com. Your personal data is permanently deleted in accordance with Saudi data protection regulations.
